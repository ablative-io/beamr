THE REPAIR — three imports the no_std build always wanted
    term/compare/bigint.rs   use std::cmp::Ordering  ->  use core::cmp::Ordering
    term/format.rs           + cfg-gated use alloc::string::String
    term/shared_binary.rs    + cfg-gated use alloc::vec::Vec

`core` needs no cfg and is the same type `std` re-exports. `alloc` DOES need
one: lib.rs gates `extern crate alloc` behind
`any(not(feature = "std"), target_arch = "wasm32")`, so a bare `use alloc::`
would break the std build. The two added imports mirror that predicate exactly.

⚠️ WHY THIS WENT UNSEEN — the mechanism outlives the bug
This leg reds STRUCTURALLY on the Linux venue (#31: the script dies before it
counts anything). The landing's own differential therefore recorded rc=3 on
BOTH arms, and that was counted as one of nine matching legs.

Two dead instruments agreeing is not a match. It is the absence of a
measurement, twice, wearing a green tick inside a 9/9 count.

A gate that reds structurally does not fail closed — it fails SILENT, and it
fails silent exactly where the thing it guards is changing. For as long as that
leg reds structurally, every landing that crosses it is ungated and looks
gated. #31's cure is promoted in its lane on the strength of this.
